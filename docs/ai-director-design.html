AI Director — Architecture Design

12 October 2026 · Miguel Werneck · Follows docs/ai-director-research.md and docs/ai-director-requirements-and-advice.md · Source of this PDF: docs/ai-director-design.html

In short: six AI agents follow a fixed recipe written in code. Some steps run at the same time. Mistakes go back to whoever made them. Nothing is paid for until I approve. The result is one Seedance video.

1. What this document is

The research said what a good AI ad needs. The advice said what to build. This shows how it fits together.

The ad is one Seedance 2.5 video with several shots inside, at a length I choose (4–30 s). The character goes in as text. The product and location go in as pictures.

The crew

Three new agents, three reused agents, two generators, and code.

1 · Interrogatornew agent
Fills in what the brief left out. Asks me only what I must decide.
2 · Directornew agent
Writes the plan: style, assets, shots. Uses the Higgsfield skill.
3 · Cinematographernew agent
Camera and light for every shot, in one call, so each shot follows the last.
4 · Describerreused agent
Describes the avatar sheet in words.
5 · Evaluatorreused agent
Scores that description.
6 · Repairerreused agent
Fixes only the failing words.
7 · Nano Banana Proimage generator
Makes the avatar, product and location pictures.
8 · Seedance 2.5video generator
Makes the one video, with sound.
+ Codenot AI
Sets the order, builds and checks the prompt, shows the price.

6. The forms agents hand each other

Agents pass fixed forms, never loose text. The next agent cannot misread a form.

// packages/contract/src/director.ts
export type Plan = {
  styleLine: string;                      // pasted word for word into the prompt
  assets: { handle: string; kind: "product" | "location" | "character"; role: string }[];
  shots:  { n: number; action: string; weight: "short" | "normal" | "long" }[];
};
export type Camera = { shot: number; framing: string; lens: string; move: CameraMove; light: string };
export type CameraMove = "static" | "slow push-in" | "pull-out" | "tracking" | "orbit" | "crane up" | "handheld";

The camera move is one slot that only takes a value from a fixed list (from Higgsfield). Two moves cannot be written there, so code can check it without an AI.

7. Checks and routing — what happens when something is wrong

Code runs yes/no checks on the prompt. No AI judges. A failed check goes back only to the step that made that part, at most 2 times. The rest of the prompt never moves.

CheckExample of a failGoes back to
Every approved shot is in the promptPlan has 4 shots, prompt has 3Director
Exactly one camera move per shot, from the list"push-in while panning left"Cinematographer (fixes that shot, keeps the others)
At most 5 reference pictures, each with a role6 pictures, or one nobody usesDirector
The shots fit the length I chose7 long shots in 10 secondsDirector
Style line and character text are identical everywhere"short brown hair" vs "dark hair"Assembler pastes it again

The final prompt the Assembler builds

@Image1 is the headphones (@headphones). @Image2 is the apartment (@apartment).

Photoreal commercial, physical cine lens, 180° shutter motion blur, natural light with soft haze,
muted palette of slate grey, warm amber and off-white. Behaviour shown in the body, never stated.

HERO (@hero): a man in his late twenties, lean build, short dark-brown curly hair, light olive skin,
grey crewneck sweater, dark trousers.

Scene: the apartment from @Image2, window on the left, front door on the right.

Shot 1: Wide shot, 24mm, static. Hero lies awake as traffic noise pours through the open window;
he presses a pillow over one ear. <traffic rumble> <a distant siren>
Shot 2: Medium close-up, 50mm, slow push-in. He reaches to the bedside table and lifts the
headphones from @Image1 over his ears in one smooth motion. <fabric rustle>
Shot 3: Close-up, 85mm, static, warm lamp from the right. The cups seal; his shoulders drop and his
eyes close for a beat. <the city noise falls to a low hum> (soft ambient synth)
Shot 4: Medium shot, 35mm, tracking. He walks out of the front door into bright morning sun, calm,
headphones on. (the synth rises gently)

Keep it subtitle-free, do not generate a logo or watermark.

Code pastes every piece from the approved plan. No model rewrites it. What I approved is what renders.

9. Build order

  1. Forms and runner, tested with fake agents. Free.
  2. Interrogator and Director, using the Higgsfield skill.
  3. Pictures and character, reusing what exists.
  4. Cinematographer, Assembler, checks and routing.
  5. Director tab.
  6. Two briefs rendered, and the write-up.
